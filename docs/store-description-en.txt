SmO2 Control: Steady-State Intensity Monitor

A data field for the Moxy muscle oxygen sensor that answers one question in
real time: am I in steady state, or is my saturation still drifting down?

The problem with absolute values

"SmO2 = 34 %" does not help you. The absolute number depends on sensor site,
adipose thickness, strap pressure and day form. What is informative is the
kinetics: how fast saturation falls at interval onset, whether it plateaus, and
how quickly it recovers.

Five states, five colours

ONSET (orange): rapid desaturation, the on-transient at interval start
ZONE 3 (red): the decline continues, no steady state exists here
ZONE 2+ (yellow): slow decline, at the upper boundary
ZONE 2 (green): flat, supply matches demand, a sustainable steady state
ZONE 1 (blue): rising, recovery or the effort is too easy

Named for the three-zone model, mapped by behaviour rather than by absolute
intensity; the kinetic names (ON-KIN / OVER / CONTROL / STEADY / REOXY) are one
setting away.

The decisive distinction is ONSET versus ZONE 3. Every hard interval begins
with a steep fall, and that fall is not yet a verdict; what separates a
sustainable interval from an overcooked one is whether the plateau arrives
afterwards. So the transient gets its own state, instead of painting every hard
interval red for a minute.

Calibrated against real data

The thresholds are measured, not guessed: across five real threshold sessions
the slope inside a plateau stays within ±0.06 %/s over a 60-second window,
while the on-transient runs past −0.23 %/s.

Adaptive display

Full and half screen. The chart is the subject: state-coloured segments with
the area beneath filled, axes labelled MIN and MAX, lap markers, a forecast
triangle. Value and state light above, rate and pace or power below.

Every smaller field gets a traffic light and one number, centred as a group. The
number is configurable (SmO2, rate of change, THb, control index), with the
rate of change on a second line where there is room.

The rate reads in %/s or %/min, and MIN and MAX cover the whole session or the
current lap. The light also carries a symbol, so the verdict never rests on hue
alone, and a colour-blind palette is available.

Session-internal calibration

Baseline median over the first minute, rolling session min/max with slow
relaxation against outliers, first lap as a reference interval, and a double
lap press to reset after re-seating the sensor.

Decoupling detection

If pace or power holds steady for 30 seconds while saturation keeps falling,
the field flags DECOUPLING: the onset of fatigue or efficiency loss, invisible
in either signal on its own.

Recording for post-analysis

Every second to the FIT file: smoothed SmO2, slope, SmO2 Control Index, state,
total haemoglobin. Every lap: desaturation rate, steepest on-kinetics slope,
minimum, maximum. All charted in Garmin Connect.

Important before first use

The Moxy must not also be paired natively in your watch's sensor list. Muscle
oxygenation was never part of the native Connect IQ sensor API, so this field
opens its own ANT channel, and a sensor holds only one; whichever side claims
it first blocks the other. Unpair the Moxy and the field finds it itself.

Invalid readings and ambient-light warnings are treated as "no data", never as
0 %. On a dropout the trend freezes rather than inventing a slope, and the
channel reconnects on its own.

Configurable

Sensor ID, smoothing, forecast horizon, steady-state window, both thresholds,
chart window, y axis, baseline duration, state naming, symbols, small-field
metric and second line, rate unit, colour-blind mode, pace, FIT recording.

To tune against your own intervals, the repository includes a tool that runs
the same computation offline on your .fit files.

Requirements

A Moxy or other ANT+ Muscle Oxygen sensor, plus a Garmin watch with ANT+:
Forerunner 245 to 970, Fenix 6 to 8, Epix Gen 2, Enduro, MARQ Gen 2 and
Edge 530 to 1050.
