SmO2 Control: Steady-State Intensity Monitor

A data field for the Moxy muscle oxygen sensor. One question, in real time:
am I in steady state, or is my saturation still drifting down?

The problem with absolute values

"SmO2 = 34 %" does not help you. The absolute number depends on sensor site,
adipose thickness, strap pressure and day form. What is informative is the
kinetics: how fast saturation falls at onset, whether it plateaus, how quickly
it recovers.

Five states, five colours

ONSET (orange): rapid desaturation, the on-transient at interval start
FALLING (red): the decline continues, no steady state exists here
DRIFTING (yellow): slow decline, demanding but still controlled
HOLDING (green): flat, supply matches demand, a sustainable steady state
RECOVER (blue): rising, recovery or the effort is too easy

The labels name the behaviour measured. The kinetic terms (ON-KIN, OVER,
CONTROL, STEADY, REOXY) are one setting away. Zone numbers are absent on
purpose: a zone is a claim about intensity, this field measures a slope.

ONSET versus FALLING is the distinction that matters. Every hard interval
begins with a steep fall, and that is not yet a verdict; what separates a
sustainable interval from an overcooked one is whether the plateau arrives
afterwards. So the transient gets its own state.

Calibrated against real data

The thresholds are measured, not guessed. Across five real threshold sessions
the slope inside a plateau stays within ±0.06 %/s over 60 seconds, while the
on-transient runs past −0.23 %/s.

Adaptive display

Full screen: value and state light on top, the chart in the middle, rate below,
and MIN / MAX / PACE as a labelled cell grid under it. Every row is as large as
its width allows, and on a bike computer the rows sit on the edges so the chart
takes the height between. It shows state-coloured segments with the area
beneath filled, gridlines, lap markers, and a forecast needle pointing in at
where the trend is heading.

Every smaller field gets a traffic light and one number, centred as a group.
The number is configurable (SmO2, rate, THb, control index), with the rate on
a second line where there is room.

The rate reads in %/s or %/min, MIN and MAX cover the session or the current
lap, and the light carries a symbol so the verdict never rests on hue alone.
There is a colour-blind palette.

Session-internal calibration

Baseline median over the first minute, rolling session min/max that relaxes
slowly against outliers, first lap as a reference, double lap to reset.

Decoupling detection

If pace or power holds steady for 30 seconds while saturation keeps falling,
the field flags decoupling: the onset of fatigue or efficiency loss, invisible
in either signal alone.

Recording for post-analysis

Every second to the FIT file: smoothed SmO2, slope, Control Index, state,
total haemoglobin. Every lap: desaturation rate, steepest on-kinetics slope,
minimum, maximum. Charted in Garmin Connect.

Important before first use

The Moxy must not also be paired natively in your watch's sensor list. Muscle
oxygenation was never part of the native Connect IQ sensor API, so this field
opens its own ANT channel, and a sensor holds only one; whichever side claims
it first blocks the other. Unpair it and the field finds it itself.

Invalid readings and ambient-light warnings count as "no data", never as 0 %.
On a dropout the trend freezes rather than inventing a slope, and the channel
reconnects on its own.

Configurable

Sensor ID, smoothing, forecast horizon, steady-state window, both thresholds,
chart window, y axis, baseline, state naming, symbols, small-field metric and
second line, rate unit, colour-blind mode, pace, FIT.

The repository includes a tool that runs the same computation offline on your
own .fit files, to tune against your own intervals.

Requirements

A Moxy or other ANT+ Muscle Oxygen sensor, plus a Garmin watch with ANT+:
Forerunner 245-970, Fenix 6-8, Epix Gen 2, Enduro, MARQ Gen 2, Edge 530-1050.
