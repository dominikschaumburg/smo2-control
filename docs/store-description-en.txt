SmO2 Control – Steady-State Intensity Monitor

A data field for the Moxy muscle oxygen sensor that answers one question in real
time: am I in steady state, or is my saturation still drifting down?

THE PROBLEM WITH ABSOLUTE VALUES

"SmO2 = 34 %" does not help you. The absolute number depends on sensor site,
adipose tissue thickness, strap pressure and day form, and is barely comparable
between sessions. What is informative is the kinetics: how fast saturation falls
at interval onset, whether it reaches a plateau, and how quickly it recovers.

FIVE STATES, FIVE COLOURS

ON-KIN (orange) – Rapid desaturation, the on-transient at interval start
OVER (red) – The decline continues, you are past the sustainable point
CONTROL (yellow) – Slow decline, demanding but controlled
STEADY (green) – Flat, supply matches demand, sustainable
REOXY (blue) – Rising, recovery or the effort is too easy

The decisive distinction is ON-KIN versus OVER. Every hard interval begins with
a steep fall, and that fall is not yet a verdict. What separates a sustainable
interval from an overcooked one is whether the plateau arrives afterwards. So
the transient gets its own state, instead of painting every hard interval red
for its first minute.

CALIBRATED AGAINST REAL DATA

The thresholds are not guesses. They are measured across five real threshold
sessions: inside a plateau the slope over a 60-second window stays within
±0.06 %/s, while the on-transient runs past −0.23 %/s. The window length was
determined the same way.

ADAPTIVE DISPLAY

The field adapts to its size, in three tiers:

Full screen – sparkline with segments coloured by state, lap markers,
calibration bands and a forecast marker, plus value, state, slope, SmO2 Control
Index and pace or power.
Half screen – value with trend arrow plus a mini sparkline.
Small field – state as the background colour, with value and trend arrow.

A colour-blind palette is available.

SESSION-INTERNAL CALIBRATION

Baseline median over the first minute, rolling session min/max with slow
relaxation to resist outliers, first lap as a reference interval, and a double
lap press as a reset after re-seating the sensor. The y axis scales
automatically to the session range rather than 0–100 %, because in practice a
Moxy lives between roughly 20 and 80 %.

DECOUPLING DETECTION

If your pace or power holds steady for 30 seconds while saturation keeps
falling, the field flags DECOUPLING. That is the onset of fatigue or efficiency
loss — and it is invisible in either signal on its own.

RECORDING FOR POST-ANALYSIS

Every second to the FIT file: smoothed SmO2, slope in %/s, SmO2 Control Index,
state and total haemoglobin, all charted in Garmin Connect.
Every lap: desaturation rate (end minus start over lap duration), steepest
on-kinetics slope, minimum and maximum. Your interval analysis comes for free.

IMPORTANT BEFORE FIRST USE

The Moxy must not also be paired natively in your watch's sensor list. Muscle
oxygenation was never part of the native Connect IQ sensor API, so this data
field opens its own ANT channel. A sensor can hold only one channel — whichever
side claims it first blocks the other. Unpair the Moxy in your watch settings
and the field will find it by itself.

Invalid readings and ambient-light warnings are treated as "no data", never as
0 %. When the sensor drops out, trend computation freezes rather than inventing
a slope, and the channel reconnects on its own.

CONFIGURABLE

Sensor ID, smoothing, forecast horizon, steady-state window, both state
thresholds, chart window, y-axis mode, baseline duration, colour-blind mode,
pace display and FIT recording.

To tune against your own intervals, the project repository includes a tool that
runs the same computation offline on .fit files.

REQUIREMENTS

A Moxy, or another sensor using the ANT+ Muscle Oxygen profile, plus a Garmin
watch with an ANT+ radio: Forerunner 245 to 970, Fenix 6 to 8, Epix Gen 2,
Enduro, MARQ Gen 2 and Edge 530 to 1050.
